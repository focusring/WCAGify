The video under **"<span lang="nl">Video over hypotheekrente</span>"** (2 min 26, Dennis Konijn, Financieel Adviseur Wonen) explains mortgage interest to camera. Most on-screen text repeats what he says, but two parts are visual only:

- **The worked example (0:55 to 1:03).** On screen: "<span lang="nl">Marktwaarde woning € 450.000</span>", a loan rising from € 360.000 to € 450.000 and the rate climbing from 3,50 % to 4,75 %. The speech says only "Hoe meer je leent ten opzichte van de marktwaarde van de woning, hoe hoger de rente. Omdat de geldverstrekker meer risico loopt." The figures are the only numbers in the video, and they appear nowhere else on the page.
- **The closing card (2:20 to 2:26):** "<span lang="nl">Meer weten? abnamro.nl/hypotheekrente</span>" and "<span lang="nl">voor ieder begin</span>", shown over the sound logo with no speech.

There is no audio description, no described version and no transcript near the player. The player's menu offers captions, speed and download, but no description. A blind viewer hears the principle but not the example that shows what it costs. The video has captions (burned in and as a track), so deaf viewers are served; this is about blind and low-vision viewers. The same gap fails 1.2.5 (Audio Description, Prerecorded).

This is a different film from the one in "The app video shows text on screen that the soundtrack never says" (page-18, page-11), which needs its own fix.

#### Recommendation

Have the presenter or a voice-over say the example, for instance "<span lang="nl">bij een woning van 450.000 euro loopt de rente op van 3,50 % bij een lening van 360.000 euro tot 4,75 % bij 450.000 euro</span>", and read the closing line. That satisfies 1.2.3 and 1.2.5. A transcript next to the player that includes the on-screen figures satisfies 1.2.3 only ([G78](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G78), [G69](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G69), [G58](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G58)).
