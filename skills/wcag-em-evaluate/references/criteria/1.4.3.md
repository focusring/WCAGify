# 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum)

Level AA · WCAG 2.0+ · Guideline 1.4 Distinguishable · Group: `visual`

Sources: [Understanding](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)
· [Spec](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum)
· [Techniques](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/#contrast-minimum)
· [Plain English](https://aaardvarkaccessibility.com/wcag-plain-english/1-4-3-contrast-minimum/)
· ACT: [afw4f7](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/act/rules/afw4f7/),
[09o5cg](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/act/rules/09o5cg/)

## Requirement

> The visual presentation of text and images of text has a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1,
> except for the following:
>
> - Large Text: Large-scale text and images of large-scale text have a contrast ratio of at least
>   3:1;
> - Incidental: Text or images of text that are part of an inactive user interface component, that
>   are pure decoration, that are not visible to anyone, or that are part of a picture that
>   contains significant other visual content, have no contrast requirement.
> - Logotypes: Text that is part of a logo or brand name has no contrast requirement.

Terms (WCAG 2.2 glossary): **contrast ratio**: "(L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), where L1 is the relative
luminance of the lighter of the colors, and L2 is the relative luminance of the darker of the
colors. [...] If no background color is specified, then white is assumed." **large scale**:
"with at least 18 point or 14 point bold [...] Font size is the size when the content is
delivered." **pure decoration**: "[...] Text is only purely decorative if the words can be
rearranged or substituted without changing their purpose."

## In plain terms

Every piece of readable text, including words rendered into an image, must stand out from whatever
is behind it: 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text. The measure is luminance only, so hue does not help; it
is set so that people with moderately low vision, colour vision deficiency or ageing eyes can read
without contrast-enhancing software. Placeholder, hover and focus text count; 4.499:1 fails.

## Applies when

- Present: any sample state that renders visible text (copy, links, buttons, labels, placeholders
  (`forms.js` fields with a `placeholder`), custom tooltips, hover and focus text, open menus and
  dialogs, text over images or gradients, inline SVG `text`) or images of text (`images.js`
  entries whose `alt`, `name` or `nearbyText` read like a banner, headline, button or badge).
- Not present: a sample state with no visible text and no images of text at all (a full-screen
  image or video without a word). Any other state is `pass` or `fail`, never `not-present`.
- Exceptions: "Large Text": 3:1 at 18 pt (24 px) or more, or 14 pt (18.67 px) at font weight 700
  or more (afw4f7), measured at viewport 1280, not at the zoom emulation (glossary Note 2).
  "Incidental": an "inactive user interface component" (visible but not operable: `disabled` or
  `aria-disabled="true"` on the widget or a `fieldset` or group ancestor, and its label), "pure
  decoration" (background lettering whose words could be swapped), text "not visible to anyone"
  (`display: none`, off-screen, clipped, the same colour as its background), a "picture that
  contains significant other visual content" (a street sign in a photo). "Logotypes": "Text that
  is part of a logo or brand name"; brand guidelines "beyond logo and logotype" are not exempt.

## axe-core

| Rule             | Decides                     | Result → outcome                            |
| ---------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `color-contrast` | text on background (afw4f7) | violation → fail (100); incomplete → step 2 |

`color-contrast` (WCAG 2.0 A & AA rule set, always run) composites the computed `color` (with
`opacity` and alpha) over the first opaque `background-color` up the tree, skips disabled widgets
and their labels, and draws the large-text line at 18 pt / 14 pt bold. Settles: every text node it
reports as a violation or a pass, in the state it ran in. Leaves open: nodes it reports as
incomplete (background image, gradient, overlapping element, pseudo-element background, foreground
alpha, complex text shadow, outside the viewport, very short text), each with its `target` in
`.notes/audit/axe/<sample>[--<state>].json`; images of text and inline SVG `text`; placeholder,
hover, focus and dark-scheme colours; [F24](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F24)
(a text colour set without a background or the reverse, "tested separately from this rule" per
afw4f7); the exemptions it cannot judge (logotype, decoration, a greyed control not marked
disabled); a [G174](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G174) contrast switch.

## Manual procedure

Work at `set viewport 1280 900` (640 and 320 change layout, not colour). Repeat steps 1 to 3 in
every recorded state (cookie dialog, open menu, expanded section, error state); each has its own
axe file. No screen reader is involved. At AAA the same steps at 7:1 / 4.5:1 decide 1.4.6 (09o5cg).

1. Read the axe result for this state: the `1.4.3` line printed by `scripts/axe-page.mjs` (`fail
color-contrast (n nodes)` or `review color-contrast (n nodes)`), then the `color-contrast`
   nodes under `violations` and `incomplete` in the JSON (`target`, `html`, and the check `data`:
   `fgColor`, `bgColor`, `contrastRatio`, `expectedContrastRatio`, `messageKey`). Test each
   violation node for the exemptions axe cannot see: a logotype or brand name (the site name in
   the header, a wordmark), pure decoration (background lettering), a control inactive for the
   user but not marked disabled; everything else is a fail at 100. Rerun with `--selector <css>`
   per region when a rule reports more nodes than the 10 it lists. Done when every violation node
   is a fail or an exemption with its reason, and every incomplete node is listed for step 2.

2. Measure what axe left incomplete, plus placeholders. The script composites the text colour
   (with `opacity` and alpha) over the `background-color` layers up to the first opaque ancestor,
   white when none is set (Note 3), reads the `::placeholder` colour of empty fields, skips SVG
   and disabled widgets, flags a background image or gradient behind the text (`bgImage`) and
   returns only what does not pass; a node whose `messageKey` says overlap or obscured goes to
   step 3 regardless. Put the incomplete `target` selectors in `sels` or keep `'body *'`. Save the
   body as `.notes/audit/contrast-1.4.3.js` (an eval script, not a probe); it keeps helpers on
   `window.__cr` for step 3, so rerun it after `reload`:

   ```bash
   agent-browser eval --stdin <<'EOF'
   (() => {
     const sels = ['body *'];
     const lin = c => { c /= 255; return c <= 0.04045 ? c / 12.92 : ((c + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4 };
     const lum = ([r, g, b]) => 0.2126 * lin(r) + 0.7152 * lin(g) + 0.0722 * lin(b);
     const rgba = s => Object.assign([0, 0, 0, 1], (s.match(/[\d.]+/g) ?? []).map(Number));
     const over = (t, u) => t.slice(0, 3).map((c, i) => c * t[3] + u[i] * (1 - t[3]));
     const ratio = (a, b) => { const [h, l] = [lum(a), lum(b)].sort((x, y) => y - x);
       return (h + 0.05) / (l + 0.05) };
     const hex = c => '#' + c.map(v => Math.round(v).toString(16).padStart(2, '0')).join('');
     window.__cr = { rgba, ratio, hex };
     const owns = el => el.placeholder ||
       [...el.childNodes].some(n => n.nodeType === 3 && n.data.trim());
     const shown = el => { const r = el.getBoundingClientRect();
       return r.width > 1 && r.height > 1 };
     const path = el => { const p = []; for (; el && el !== document.body; el = el.parentElement) {
       const i = [...el.parentElement.children].filter(c => c.tagName === el.tagName).indexOf(el);
       p.unshift(el.id ? '#' + CSS.escape(el.id) : el.localName + ':nth-of-type(' + (i + 1) + ')') }
       return p.join(' > ') };
     return [...new Set(sels.flatMap(s => [...document.querySelectorAll(s)]))]
       .filter(el => !el.closest('svg,:disabled,[aria-disabled=true]') && shown(el) && owns(el))
       .map(el => {
         const cs = getComputedStyle(el), layers = []; let alpha = 1, image = null, opaque = false;
         for (let n = el; n && !opaque; n = n.parentElement) { const s = getComputedStyle(n);
           if (!image && s.backgroundImage !== 'none') image = n.localName;
           const b = rgba(s.backgroundColor); if (b[3] > 0) layers.unshift(b); opaque = b[3] === 1 }
         for (let n = el; n; n = n.parentElement) alpha *= +getComputedStyle(n).opacity;
         let bg = [255, 255, 255]; for (const l of layers) bg = over(l, bg);
         const fgc = rgba(el.placeholder && !el.value
           ? getComputedStyle(el, '::placeholder').color : cs.color);
         fgc[3] *= alpha; const fg = over(fgc, bg), ra = ratio(fg, bg);
         const pt = parseFloat(cs.fontSize) * 0.75, need = pt >= 18 ||
           (pt >= 14 && (parseInt(cs.fontWeight) || 400) >= 700) ? 3 : 4.5;
         return { sel: path(el), fg: hex(fg), bg: hex(bg), pt: +pt.toFixed(1), need,
           ratio: +ra.toFixed(3), bgImage: image, passes: image ? null : ra >= need,
           text: String(el.value || el.placeholder || el.innerText).trim().slice(0, 40) };
       }).filter(x => x.passes !== true);
   })()
   EOF
   ```

   `passes: false` is a measured fail on a solid background (compared unrounded); `passes: null`
   goes to step 3; `fg` equal to `bg` is text nobody sees. Done when every entry is classified.

3. Decide text over images, gradients and overlaps
   ([F83](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/failures/F83)): for each `passes: null` entry
   and each incomplete node whose `messageKey` names a background image, gradient, overlap or
   obscuring, run `scrollintoview "<sel>"`, `screenshot "<sel>" <evidence path>` and read the PNG.
   A solid scrim, a dark text shadow or a letter outline counts (Note 5: a narrow border is the
   letter, a wide halo is background). For a CSS gradient take the `rgb()` stops from
   `get styles "<sel>"` (`background-image`) and rate each against the text colour through
   `eval --stdin`: `window.__cr.ratio(window.__cr.rgba('<stop>'), window.__cr.rgba('<fg>'))`.
   Fail when no region behind the letters reaches `need` (afw4f7) or when some letters sit on a
   failing region (F83); pass when every letter sits on a region that reaches `need`;
   `cannot-tell` when the image did not load. Done when each candidate has a verdict.

4. Measure colours that appear only on hover, on focus or in the site's own dark scheme. For one
   control of each visual kind in `snapshot -i` (menu link, body link, primary and secondary
   button, tab, field) and every `focusables.js` entry with `focusStyleChanged` true, run
   `scrollintoview "<sel>"`, `hover "<sel>"`, `eval --stdin < .notes/audit/contrast-1.4.3.js`
   with `sels` set to that selector, then `focus "<sel>"` and the same call; entries that appear
   now are hover or focus fails. Then `get styles body`, `set media dark`, `reload`,
   `wait --load networkidle`, `get styles body` again: a changed `background-color` or `color` is
   an author-shipped dark scheme, so run `scripts/axe-page.mjs` with `--state dark` and steps 1
   to 3, then `set media light`. An in-page control named theme, dark or contrast in `snapshot -i`
   gets steps 1 to 3 after `click` too, but a lower-contrast result there is a note, not a fail,
   when the default presentation passes and the control returns to it (Understanding: "This is not
   a violation of this Success Criterion, provided there is a mechanism that will return to the
   required values set out here"). Done when hover, focus and every shipped scheme have entries.

5. Check images of text and inline SVG text. Run `images.js` and keep entries whose `alt`, `name`
   or `nearbyText` read like rendered words and `kind: 'svg'` entries that draw words;
   `get count "svg text"` finds SVG text the probe skipped. For each candidate run
   `screenshot "<selector>" <path>` and read the PNG: an image of text (judge letter and
   background colours from the PNG; for SVG read `fill` with `get styles "svg text"` and rate it
   with the step 3 call), a picture with significant other visual content (exempt), or a logo or
   brand name (exempt; signs: `inLink` to the home page, `alt` equal to the site name). Done when
   every candidate is classified.

6. Check the page canvas and any contrast switch. F24 fails when a text colour is set but no
   background, or the reverse; the usual case is `html` and `body` left on the user-agent
   background while the page sets `color`: `get styles html` and `get styles body` both with
   `background-color` `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)` and `background-image` `none` while `body` has a `color`
   other than `rgb(0, 0, 0)`. [G148](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G148) (no
   colours set at all) is a pass. A high-contrast switch (G174; a control named contrast in
   `snapshot -i`) matters when the default presentation fails: it must then pass step 2 itself, and
   `click` it and rerun steps 1 to 3 to see whether the switched presentation passes everywhere.
   Done when both are recorded.

## Deciding

- Fail (certainty ≥ 90): a `color-contrast` violation node that is not an exemption (100); a step 2
  entry with `passes: false` (95); a placeholder, hover, focus or `prefers-color-scheme: dark` entry
  below `need` (90); when the default presentation fails, a G174 switch whose own text fails, or
  whose switched presentation also fails (90).
- Fail (70–89): text over an image, gradient or overlap where the step 3 screenshot and stop
  ratios show no region behind the letters reaching `need` (F83) (85), or only some letters on a
  failing region (75); inline SVG text whose `fill` falls short (80); an image of text whose
  letters and background, judged from the PNG, fall short (75); a transparent `html` and `body`
  canvas with a set text colour (F24) (75); a greyed but operable control whose text fails (70).
- Needs confirmation (50–69): a text node that may be a logotype (60): ask whether brand
  guidelines fix its colours; text in a photo that may be an image of text (55): ask what the
  image is for; lettering that may be pure decoration (55): ask whether the words carry meaning;
  a greyed control not marked disabled (55): ask whether it is inactive; an unloaded image (50).
- Pass: every visible text node and image of text in every recorded state, including hover, focus,
  placeholders and each author-shipped scheme, reaches 4.5:1 (3:1 when large) by axe or by step 2, 3
  or 5, or is an exemption with its reason written down; the canvas has a set background or no
  colours at all (G148); or, where the default presentation fails, a G174 switch and its target
  presentation both pass.
- Not present: only a sample state with no visible text and no images of text.
- False positives to avoid: disabled controls and their labels are exempt; text hidden from sight
  (`display: none`, clipped `.sr-only` text, 1:1 colour) has no visual presentation; a logo or
  wordmark is exempt even as a link; a text shadow or outline counts as letter or background, so a
  light heading with a dark shadow on a photo can pass; a nominal pass on a thin font stays a pass;
  user-forced colours are not the author's pair; a user-selected alternate theme with lower
  contrast is not a fail when the default presentation passes and the switch returns to it; 4.5:1
  to 7:1 fails 1.4.6 only.

## Reporting

- File under: 1.4.3. Non-text parts of the same control (icons, borders, focus rings) go to
  1.4.11; an image of text that reads fine but should be real text to 1.4.5; a link told apart
  only by colour to 1.4.1; a control greyed by CSS but not programmatically disabled to 4.1.2. One
  issue per colour pair or text style, naming every sample and state it occurs in.
- Title patterns: "Grey body text on the white background is too faint", "Placeholder text in the
  search field has too little contrast", "White heading over the hero image is unreadable"
- Severity: High when body copy, headings, navigation, labels, button text or error messages fail,
  or the failing style repeats across the product; Medium when secondary text (helper text,
  captions, placeholders, footer links) fails, or only one state (hover, focus, dark scheme) or
  part of an image background fails; Low when a peripheral line misses by a small margin.
- Type: Design by default; Technical when code causes it (a missing canvas background, F24, an
  `opacity` or overlay that fades text, a `::placeholder` rule); Content when editors chose them.
- Evidence: an element screenshot of the text at its rendered size, `highlight "<sel>"` before a
  page screenshot, the measured pair and ratio stated next to it ("#777777 on #eeeeee = 3.86:1,
  needs 4.5:1"); a GIF via `record start` / `record stop` when the colour appears only on hover.
- Recommendation: change the text or background colour until the pair reaches 4.5:1, or 3:1 for
  large text ([G18](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G18),
  [G145](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Techniques/general/G145)); set both colours or neither (F24,
  G148); a solid scrim behind text on images (F83); a switch only as a last resort (G174). Example:
  `color: #767676` on `#ffffff` gives 4.54:1, `#595959` gives 7.0:1.
- Related: 1.4.6 at AAA (4.5:1 to 7:1 fails only there); 1.4.11 for the non-text parts of the same
  component; 2.4.7 for the focus indicator itself; 1.4.1 when colour is also the only cue.
